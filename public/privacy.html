ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD

Privacy policy — mobile app

This policy explains what personal information ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD holds about you as a member, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the ILBORU SACCOS mobile app for iPhone and Android and the member portal on our website.

Who is responsible

ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD, a registered savings and credit cooperative society in Tanzania, is responsible for the information described here. You can reach us at support@ias.co.tz or at the SACCOS office.

Information we hold

Membership and identity details

Your name, membership number, date you joined, phone number, email address, national identification details, address, and the next of kin and heirs you have nominated. This information is collected when you join the SACCOS at our office. You cannot create an account inside the app — the app only shows you information the SACCOS already holds about you.

Your financial records with the SACCOS

Your savings balance and transaction history, share capital, dividends allocated to you, loans you have taken, repayments, and loans you have guaranteed for other members. These records are created by SACCOS operations, not by the app.

Sign-in information

Your username and password. Passwords are stored in an encrypted, hashed form by our authentication provider and cannot be read by us or by SACCOS staff.

Device and technical information

If you allow notifications, we store a notification token issued by your phone's operating system so we can deliver alerts to your device. We also keep security and audit logs of activity on your account — the kind of record any financial institution must keep to protect your money.

What never leaves your phone

Face ID and Touch ID / fingerprint. These are handled entirely by your phone's operating system. We never receive, see, or store your fingerprint or face data. The app is only told whether your phone accepted you.

Your 4-digit PIN. Your PIN is never sent to us and is never stored anywhere on our systems. Only a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of it is kept in your phone's own secure storage — the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore — so that even that stored value cannot be turned back into your PIN. Removing the app, or turning the PIN off, removes it.

How we use your information

  • To let you sign in securely and see your own accounts
  • To operate your savings, loans, shares and dividends as a member of the cooperative
  • To send you alerts about activity on your account, loan decisions and SACCOS announcements
  • To keep accurate accounting and audit records, as a cooperative is required to do
  • To protect your account against unauthorised access and fraud
  • To meet our obligations under Tanzanian cooperative, financial and anti-money-laundering law

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

Who else can see your information

Your information is shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • SACCOS staff, to the extent their role requires — for example a teller recording your deposit or a loan officer assessing your application. Staff access is limited by role and is logged.
  • Other members, in limited ways you would expect. If you ask another member to guarantee your loan, they are told what they are guaranteeing. If you take part in a savings challenge, your name and position appear in the standings shown to members.
  • Service providers who run our systems on our behalf — our database and authentication hosting provider, and Google's Firebase service, which delivers notifications to your phone. They process data only on our instructions and are required to protect it to the same standard we do.
  • Regulators, auditors, and authorities, where the law requires us to disclose information.

How long we keep it

We keep your membership and financial records for as long as you are a member, and afterwards for as long as Tanzanian cooperative and financial record-keeping rules require. Security and audit logs are kept for a limited period appropriate to their purpose.

Your choices and your rights

  • See what we hold. You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it. If something is wrong — your phone number, your next of kin — tell us and we will fix it.
  • Ask us to delete it. You can ask us to delete your personal information. We will do so except where we are legally required to keep certain financial records; we will tell you plainly what we must keep and why.
  • Close your membership. Membership is a legal relationship with the cooperative, so it is closed at the office rather than in the app. Contact us and we will take you through it.
  • Turn off notifications at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Turn off Face ID, Touch ID or your PIN at any time in the app. Signing in with your password always works.

To exercise any of these, email support@ias.co.tz or visit the SACCOS office. We may need to confirm your identity first, to make sure we are not giving your information to somebody else.

Children

The app is intended for SACCOS members. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and change the date below. Significant changes will also be communicated to members.
