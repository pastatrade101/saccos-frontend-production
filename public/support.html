Mobile app support

Help for members using the ILBORU SACCOS app on iPhone and Android.

Contact us

support@ias.co.tz

Email us with your full name and membership number and we will get back to you. You are also welcome to visit the SACCOS office during working hours, where our staff can help you in person.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

The app is for registered members of ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD. Sign in with the username or email address the SACCOS office issued to you, together with your password. If you are a member but have never received sign-in details, contact us and the office will set up your access.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and enter your email address. We will send you a link to set a new password. The email comes from info@ias.co.tz — if you do not see it within a few minutes, please check your spam or junk folder.

Fingerprint, Face ID or my PIN is not working

Sign in once with your password, then turn quick sign-in back on from the app. If your phone has just been restarted, updated, or had a new fingerprint or face added, your device may ask for your password once more before quick sign-in will work again. You can also set a 4-digit PIN as an alternative.

My deposit is not showing in the app

Deposits appear once the SACCOS office has recorded them in the books. If a payment you have made is still missing after that, contact us with the date and the amount and we will check it for you.

How do I get my statement?

Open your savings account in the app and choose Share. Your statement is prepared as a PDF that you can save to your phone, print, or send on to someone else.

Something in the app does not look right

Please contact us and describe what you were doing and what you saw. A screenshot helps us a great deal. Never send us your password.

Your account and your data

Accounts are created and managed by the SACCOS office, not inside the app. If you want to close your membership, or ask what personal information we hold about you and have it removed, please email support@ias.co.tz or visit the office. Some records must be kept for a period of time to meet Tanzanian financial and cooperative regulations, and we will explain clearly what applies to your case.

Kwa Kiswahili

Programu hii ni kwa ajili ya wanachama waliosajiliwa wa ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD. Kwa msaada wowote, tuandikie support@ias.co.tz ukiweka jina lako kamili na namba yako ya uanachama, au tembelea ofisi ya SACCOS wakati wa saa za kazi.

Nimesahau nenosiri

Gusa Forgot password? kwenye ukurasa wa kuingia, kisha weka barua pepe yako. Tutakutumia kiungo cha kuweka nenosiri jipya. Barua pepe hutoka info@ias.co.tz — kama huioni, angalia kwenye spam au junk.

Alama ya kidole au PIN haifanyi kazi

Ingia mara moja kwa nenosiri lako, kisha uwashe tena njia ya haraka ya kuingia. Kama simu yako imezimwa na kuwashwa upya, imeboreshwa, au umeongeza alama mpya ya kidole au uso, simu inaweza kukuuliza nenosiri mara moja kabla haijafanya kazi tena.

Amana yangu haionekani

Amana huonekana baada ya ofisi ya SACCOS kuiingiza kwenye vitabu. Kama bado haionekani, tuandikie ukieleza tarehe na kiasi, nasi tutaifuatilia.

Ninapataje taarifa ya akaunti yangu?

Fungua akaunti yako ya akiba kwenye programu kisha uchague Share. Taarifa yako itaandaliwa kama PDF unayoweza kuhifadhi, kuichapisha, au kuituma.

Kufunga uanachama au kuondoa taarifa zako

Akaunti zinafunguliwa na ofisi ya SACCOS, si ndani ya programu. Kama unataka kufunga uanachama wako au kuuliza kuhusu taarifa zako binafsi, tuandikie support@ias.co.tz au tembelea ofisi. Baadhi ya kumbukumbu hulazimika kuhifadhiwa kwa muda kwa mujibu wa sheria za fedha na ushirika za Tanzania.
