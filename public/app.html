ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD

Your SACCOS, in your pocket

Check your savings, follow your loans and see your dividends from your phone — without queuing at the branch.

What you can do

See your savings

Your balance the moment it changes, and every deposit and withdrawal in a clear running statement.

Statements as PDF

Prepare your statement as a PDF you can save, print, or send on — straight from your phone.

Follow your loans

Every loan you are running: the balance, the schedule, what is due next, and each repayment as it lands. Applications are made at the SACCOS office or on the member portal.

Your dividends

See what the SACCOS allocated to you, when it was paid, and how it was worked out.

Shares and dividends

Track your share capital as it grows, and see your dividends the moment they are allocated.

Instant alerts

Know whenever money moves on your account, plus SACCOS announcements.

Savings challenges

Take part in savings challenges with fellow members and follow the standings day by day.

Fast, private sign-in

Unlock with your fingerprint or face, or a 4-digit PIN you set yourself. Available in English and Kiswahili.

Who it is for

The app is for registered members of ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD. You sign in with the username and password the SACCOS office issues to you.

If you are a member but have never received sign-in details, or you would like to join the SACCOS, visit the office and our staff will set you up.

Get the app

The ILBORU SACCOS app is available for iPhone and Android.

Members who need help installing it or signing in for the first time can contact us at support@ias.co.tz or visit the SACCOS office during working hours.

Kwa Kiswahili

SACCOS yako, mfukoni mwako. Angalia akiba yako, omba mkopo, na fuatilia gawio lako ukiwa na simu yako — bila kupanga foleni tawini.

Kwa kutumia programu hii unaweza kuona salio lako likibadilika papo hapo, kupata taarifa ya akaunti yako kama PDF, kuomba mkopo na kufuatilia hatua zake, kudhamini mwanachama mwenzako, kufuatilia hisa na gawio lako, na kupata taarifa mara pesa zinapoingia au kutoka.

Programu hii ni kwa ajili ya wanachama waliosajiliwa wa ILBORU ALUMNI SACCOS LTD. Unaingia kwa jina la mtumiaji na nenosiri unalopewa na ofisi ya SACCOS. Kama wewe ni mwanachama lakini hujapata taarifa za kuingia, au ungependa kujiunga, tembelea ofisi yetu.

Inapatikana kwa iPhone na Android. Kwa msaada, tuandikie support@ias.co.tz.
